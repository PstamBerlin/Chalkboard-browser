New in 1.1: the tokens (coming soon; 1.0 ignores them)

--mesh
The window's background, and the new tab page's. One of aurora (the violet mesh, dark at the top and bright at the bottom), aurora-light (Aurora's pale version), wash (a soft gradient across the top, made from --wash-a and --wash-b) or none (the flat 1.0 window). A word, never CSS.
--wash-a--wash-b
The two colours of a wash, left to right. Any colour.
--frost
The fill of the frosted glass bars and cards. Any colour, see-through allowed, like rgba(22,10,52,.42). A board without glass sets it the same as --bg.
--frost-blur
How much the glass blurs what is behind it, in px, from 0px to 50px. 0px turns the glass off and the bars go back to --bg.
--accent-ink
The text drawn on the accent (--stroke), on selected buttons. Any colour that reads on the accent.
--display
The font for titles and numbers. A font list, like "Unbounded", "Manrope", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif. --font is still the font for everything else.
--grain
How strong the built in grain is over the background, a number from 0 to 1. 0 is no grain.
--tabstyle
The tab you are on. pill (a capsule with a dark fill and a gradient edge), glow (a soft accent glow under it) or outline (exactly the 1.0 tab).
--tab-edge-a--tab-edge-b
The pill's edge, running left to right from the first colour to the second. Any colour.
--tabglow
An older name from while 1.1 was being made: on or off. Still read, as --tabstyle glow or outline. New boards should use --tabstyle.

The new tab page only takes values without < > { } ; or \ in them, nothing that loads a web address, and at most 200 characters, so keep values plain.
